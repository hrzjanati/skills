# startup-planning

## Workflow and rules

Identify target users, problem, existing alternatives and measurable success. Separate MVP from later work. Map journeys, data sensitivity, revenue assumptions and operational costs. Write feature acceptance criteria including error and permission cases. Prefer reversible decisions and a small vertical slice. Do not invent market evidence.

## Completion

Report what changed, checks actually performed, failures and unresolved risks. Do not claim success without evidence.
